Model Responses

Original model outputs to the Nordland essay assignment, each published with its provenance and test conditions.

  • Responses collected: 0
  • Prompt: Fixed, identical for every run
Test conditions

Every model receives the same input: the neutral case record and its broad essay assignment (≤ 1,500 words; charts, tables and diagrams permitted). No evaluator interpretations, rubric or examples are supplied. Outputs are published without editing.

The three essays on the Rubric page are constructed calibration examples, not model responses, and are deliberately not listed here.
