Score a Response

Score any published response on the five official dimensions. Scores, anchors, rationales and supporting excerpts are recorded together so every score can be traced to its evidence.

  • Rubric: Evaluator Interpretations and Assessment Criteria §5
  • Scoring: Manual; nothing is scored automatically

How evaluations are saved

Your work stays in this browser: a draft is kept in local storage until you clear it, and nothing is sent anywhere. Nothing you do here changes the published site or the GitHub repository. To keep an evaluation, download the JSON record and the readable report. To publish it, add the JSON record to data/evaluations.json in the repository and rebuild.

Response text
Scoring record

Level 4 is not an originality prize. An unexpected interpretation is welcome only if defensible; an insightful conventional interpretation may also merit 4. A score of 4 in any dimension requires a short, passage-specific explanation of how the response exceeded level 3. Anchors are quoted from the rubric document §5.

I. Critical analysis — identifying the problem that merits inquiry

Did the essay determine what requires understanding, rather than simply adopt the most prominent statistic?

    II. Conceptual reasoning — distinguishing and relating meaningful concepts

    Do distinctions clarify the phenomenon, rather than decorate the argument?

      III. Consequential pattern recognition and interpretation — linking evidence

      Has the model discovered consequential links or exceptions, rather than merely collected facts?

        IV. Evidentiary discipline — calibrating inference and seeking tests

        Does it know what the evidence can and cannot show, and what would change its mind?

          V. Substantive judgment — prioritizing and reaching defensible insight

          Did it decide what matters most, on sound grounds, rather than conclude that everything matters?
