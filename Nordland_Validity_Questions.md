## Validity questions and research priorities

The project must substantiate two claims separately: **the Nordland record is an internally coherent, genuinely open-ended problem**, and **the scoring process measures demonstrated conceptual reasoning rather than conformity to an evaluator's expectations**.

### Case and evidence validity

- **Numerical integrity:** Do the 16 offices' claim counts, group shares, and weighted denial rates reconcile with nationwide figures after rounding? A preliminary audit of the case record finds approximately 150,000 claims, a 19.92% minority-background share, and weighted initial denial rates of approximately 18.29% and 8.94%. Keep a reproducible verification script alongside the source.
- **Units and populations:** Separate claims from people, office data from individual cases, resident attitudes from benefit applications, minority home language from minority background, and name-cue résumés from actual claimant identities.
- **Causal scope:** Distinguish the randomized translated-notice comparison from observational office differences and proposed multi-institutional pathways.
- **Genuine alternatives:** Can administrative design, case composition, language access, historical continuity or discontinuity, employment conditions, and belonging support distinct plausible accounts? Which records would distinguish them?
- **Counterexamples:** Does a response use Office 6 and Office 12 thoughtfully, rather than treating them as automatic proof or disproof of a theory?
- **Sampling and missing evidence:** Do the voluntary website poll, lack of appeals data, unobserved eligibility, repeated claims, and missing person-level linkages change the interpretation?

### Construct and scoring validity

- **Self-directed inquiry:** Is the model deciding what to investigate, or merely following a checklist?
- **Interpretive plurality:** Can very different well-supported accounts score equally high? Can an unanticipated insight receive full credit?
- **Dimension separation:** Are scores for problem formulation, conceptual reasoning, relationship discovery, evidentiary discipline, and substantive judgment justified independently?
- **Top-score integrity:** Does a rating of 4 represent a genuinely consequential analytical step beyond a strong 3, rather than polished prose or novelty alone?
- **Equivalent media:** Are diagrams, prioritizations, concise essays, and text-heavy outputs evaluated by the same substantive standards?
- **Rater and run variability:** Do ratings agree across independent assessors, and do model patterns persist across repeated unassisted runs?
- **Exposure risk:** Could a public model retrieve evaluator notes? Maintain new held-out cases for subsequent validity research.

### Immediate research sequence

1. Preserve a reproducible case-table numerical audit and an evidence/denominator ledger.
2. Confirm that the case page contains only the neutral record and broad prompt.
3. Collect actual unedited model completions and metadata; distinguish them explicitly from constructed calibration examples.
4. Independently assign five scores with cited passages and reasons, then compare strong and weak arguments.
5. Audit accessibility, links, score arithmetic, and consistency before public presentation.
6. Expand to additional raters, repeated runs, and held-out cases only when the initial protocol is demonstrably coherent.

**Reporting boundary:** The pilot can demonstrate a method for scoring written problem formulation and judgment. It cannot establish internal model cognition, a uniquely correct explanation of Nordland, or stable general-purpose reasoning ability from one small case.
